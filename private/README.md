# private/

Internal material. **Gitignored, permanently.** Nothing in here is committed,
staged, published, or quoted at length into any committed file.

This repo is expected to become public. This directory is what makes it safe to
work with internal material anyway.

## What goes here

**`references/`**: other internal Microsoft illustration systems. The artwork,
the specs, the internal documentation, the screenshots.

**`notes/`**: working notes, survey output, call transcripts, anything with an
internal URL or a colleague's unpublished work in it.

## The rule about writing things down

Analysis derived from this material can be committed to `references/`, but only
as **original writing**. Describe what a system does and why the approach works.
Do not paste internal documentation, do not embed internal artwork, and do not
link to internal-only URLs.

The test: could this paragraph appear in a public conference talk. If yes, it
can go in `references/`. If no, it stays here.

When it is unclear, it stays here and Mason decides.
