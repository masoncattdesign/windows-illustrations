# <System name>

One paragraph on what this system is and who it serves. Skip the marketing
language.

## Structure

How is an illustration decomposed? What are the named parts, if any? Is the
decomposition enforced by tooling or by convention?

## Color

How many colors, chosen how? Is there a role model, or are colors literal? Can
an illustration be rethemed, and if so what stays fixed?

## Scale and crops

What canvas sizes exist, and are the crops systematic or per-illustration?
What happens at small sizes?

## Composition

Can new illustrations be built from existing parts, or is every one drawn from
scratch? If parts are reused, what is the unit of reuse?

## Tooling

What is automated, what is manual, and where does the system break down at
volume?

## What it gets right

The one or two things worth stealing. Be specific about the mechanism, not the
outcome.

## What it gets wrong

Where it does not hold up, and why. Constraints it accepted that we do not have
to.

## Relevance to us

Direct implications for the anatomy, the color model, or the Builder. If there
are none, say so. Not every system needs to influence ours.
